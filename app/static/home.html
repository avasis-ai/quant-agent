    Load & index

    Built to traverse.

    The FalkorDB bulk loader imports the graph in 30 seconds. Range indexes find addresses; full-text indexes find coin names and symbols.

    Algorithms & access

    A bounded graph, by design.

    algo.WCC builds the rings. algo.SPpaths traces connections. Fixed, parameterized Cypher runs through GRAPH.RO_QUERY with per-query timeouts capped at 15 seconds.

    What the graph can’t tell you.

    History, not a prediction. The graph signals added no predictive value once early trading was known (AUC change within ±0.01). Sniff explains who is behind a coin, not what its price will do.

    A snapshot, with gaps. Newer launches aren’t included. A missing funder can mean missing coverage. Early-buyer data starts on 8 October. Nothing here is financial advice.
