# QuantBase public claims and policy summaries

These summaries describe public material reviewed on 18 October 2026. They are
knowledge sources for Sniff's company brain, not a replacement for the linked
policies. No customer records, account data or private operating notes are used.
Different public pages can describe different product versions. Keep each source's
date and context rather than silently treating an older page as the latest offer.

## Paper and live are separate

The current public homepage describes Sniff's strategy tests and paper runs.
Paper mode uses play money, cannot touch a wallet, and labels its results paper.
Live trading on the user's own wallet is described as opening soon. Do not claim
that a paper result is a real fill or that customer live execution is available.

Source: https://quantbase.live/#paper and https://quantbase.live/#faq

## History and research claims

Sniff's graph investigation describes public launch history. It does not predict
returns or call a coin safe. Its published research found that graph features
added no measurable predictive accuracy after early trading features were known:
the AUC change was within ±0.01 on the reported experiment. The result is a reason
to explain operator history rather than promise profitable picks.

Source: [published research in the Sniff blog](BLOG.md#the-honest-part-it-explains-it-doesnt-predict).

## Advice, execution and uncertainty

The public terms describe measurement, computation and record keeping rather than
investment advice, signals or a promise of profit. Historical rates and backtests
can fail in a live market. The documented exit coach reports when a user-selected
rule fires; that older product description says it places no orders. Its alerts
can be delayed, missed or wrong, and quoted prices omit fees and slippage.

Source: https://quantbase.live/docs/terms/ (sections 1, 2, 2a and 7).

## Wallets and private keys

The public terms distinguish signing a wallet sign-in message from signing a
transaction or granting spending approval. They say the evidence product does
not ask for a private key or seed phrase. These statements are about the product
documented there; they do not prove the behaviour of a proposed live trading mode.

Source: https://quantbase.live/docs/terms/ (sections 2a and 2b).

## Privacy and public knowledge

The public privacy policy describes account, research, coach and billing data
used to provide those services. It says the service does not sell data for
marketing, collect private keys, or store card details. Account data and public
market observations have different scopes: public market data is shared, while
account-scoped data is isolated. The company brain ingests only the explicitly
allowlisted public documents. Sniff's investigation memory stores random session
tokens and scanned public evidence, never visitor question text, keys or network
addresses.

Source: https://quantbase.live/docs/privacy/ (what is stored, what is not
collected, isolation between accounts). The original page contains contact
addresses and is refused by the company brain's PII scanner; this summary omits
those contact details and does not grant permission to ingest private records.

## Payment and availability

The public terms describe recurring plans, cancellation at any time with access
until the paid period ends, and refunds if the service does not perform as
documented. They state no uptime guarantee and warn that third-party data can be
late or wrong. For prices and credits, use the current public pricing section,
with its fetch date, rather than assuming the older plan documentation is current.

Source: https://quantbase.live/docs/terms/ (sections 5 and 7), and
https://quantbase.live/#pricing.
